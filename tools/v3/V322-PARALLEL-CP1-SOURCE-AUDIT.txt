AUDITED ATTRIBUTION
- Ordinary pre-transition frames show Mechanics at roughly 1.37 ms median and
  1.96 ms p95 in the retained current-family run.
- LuaSync is larger in aggregate, but its input/player callbacks are latency
  sensitive and its delayed actions mutate arbitrary world state. LuaWait after
  render is effectively zero, so multiworker Lua is not the first target.
- UpdateTraversal is also material, but it is OSG-owned. Automatic threading
  remains locked and no global OSG mode is changed.
- Broad Actor::update and CharacterController::update contain AI, inventory,
  animation, world, sound, physics, Lua, and gameplay-event mutations. They are
  not admitted to workers.

CP1 BOUNDARY
- Main thread captures immutable actor collision-prediction inputs.
- Existing engine WorkQueue performs pairwise numeric candidate preparation.
- Workers never dereference Ptr, Movement, AI, OSG, physics, Lua, or world APIs.
- Main thread performs LOS/awareness validation and deterministic actor-order
  commit, including all steering writes and zTurn calls.
- Fewer than 12 actors, zero configured background threads, a missing queue, or
  a disabled switch falls through to the exact legacy serial implementation.

KNOWN SEMANTIC DELTA
- The legacy loop lets a later actor observe an earlier actor's corrected
  desired movement in the same frame. Mode141 freezes all desired movement at
  batch start. This is deterministic but requires explicit traffic/pathing
  validation and is not promotable on timing alone.

FORBIDDEN / UNCHANGED
- No OSG threading-mode change.
- No scenegraph, physics-world, Lua-state, AI-sequence, inventory, or gameplay
  event mutation from workers.
- No DatabasePager ready-queue change and no ICO policy change.
- No V3.19 instancing, V3.20 broad update skip, state sorter, PBR, shadow,
  view-distance, full-body first-person, or VRAM policy change.
- Dormant V3.22 CP1/CP2 renderer switches remain off in Mode141.

RUNTIME GATES
1. Mode135 versus Mode141 with the frozen save/mod/settings route.
2. Verify actor traffic, door approaches, follow/wander/combat pursuit, crowd
   crossings, dead-body avoidance, and repeated cell transitions.
3. Require a credible >=1 ms win, preferably >=2 ms on the targeted workload,
   without worse p99 wall time, transition tails, correctness, or VRAM.
4. Inspect OPENMW_V3_WORKQUEUE_FILE and OPENMW_V3_RENDER_FILE alongside
   v3-frames.csv; a queue-wait regression is a rejection even if mean Mechanics
   improves.
